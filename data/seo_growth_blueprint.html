Business & Product Context

What Databook is, who it serves, and why SEO matters right now

▼

What is Databook?

Databook is a B2B SaaS reporting tool built specifically for Cin7Core inventory management system users. It solves a real, painful problem: Cin7Core's native exports are messy, have junk rows at the top, and combining data across multiple exports requires hours of manual Excel work every month.

Databook lets users upload their Cin7Core CSV/Excel exports, pick the exact fields they need, and download a clean, combined report in under 60 seconds — no Excel gymnastics required.

💡
Core Pain Point (Evidence from codebase): "We were spending 3–4 hours every month pulling together our stock report from Cin7Core. Databook does it in under a minute." — Testimonial from an Australian e-commerce brand (routes/index.tsx, line 418–420)

Revenue Model

  • Free tier: 2 file uploads, first 50 rows, dashboard only
  • Pro tier ($29/month): Unlimited uploads, all rows, CSV export, unlimited templates
  • Payment: Razorpay subscription billing (India-origin payment gateway)
  • Referral codes: Discount system exists for partner/referral growth

Platform Roadmap (Evidence from codebase)

  • ✅ Cin7Core — Live (API + CSV upload)
  • ✅ CSV/Excel uploads — Live
  • 🕐 Xero — Coming soon (locked in UI)
  • 🕐 ShipStation — Coming soon (locked in UI)
  • 🕐 Cin7Core API automation — Coming soon (Pro feature)
  • 🕐 Scheduled reports — Coming soon (Pro feature)

SEO Opportunity Assessment

⚠️
Critical Launch Risk: The site is not indexation-ready. The two highest-value pages (Homepage + Pricing) are not being discovered efficiently by Google because there is no sitemap.xml and no robots.txt. Auth pages (/signin, /signup, /onboarding, /app/*) are fully indexable — Google may discover and attempt to index them, wasting crawl budget and damaging brand trust.

Business & Organic Growth Analysis

Where growth comes from, where it is blocked, and what is being missed

▼
📌
Strategic Frame (Source: 2026 Strategic Roadmap — Pipeline-Led SEO.docx): In 2026, organic traffic volume is a vanity metric. Databook must shift from a traffic engine to a pipeline engine — measuring SQLs and MRR from organic, not sessions. AI Overviews reduce top-listing CTR by ~34.5%; informational query clicks drop ~61%. Meanwhile, 37–40% of B2B buyers start research in LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude).

Business Objectives (Inferred from Codebase + Strategy Docs)

  • Acquire Cin7Core users frustrated with manual Excel reporting (primary ICP)
  • Convert free → Pro ($29/mo) via unlimited rows, CSV export, templates (plans.ts, pricing.tsx)
  • Establish category authority for "Cin7Core reporting" before competitors enter niche
  • Build integration ecosystem — Xero, ShipStation roadmap visible in platform.tsx
  • Reduce CAC through organic vs paid acquisition
  • Enable referral growth — referral code system exists (pricing.tsx)
  • Connect search to revenue via GSC → CRM attribution (MCP/QLA framework)
  • Win AI discovery layer — become cited "consensus answer" for Cin7Core reporting
