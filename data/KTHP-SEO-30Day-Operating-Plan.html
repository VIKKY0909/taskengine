The GEO vs SEO Shift

Traditional SEO targets Google ranking positions. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) targets citations inside conversational AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini — which now capture 30–40% of hospitality discovery intent.

Heritage Knowledge Base (HKB)

Build a structured, citable database of KTHP facts — founding dates, noble genealogy, architectural measurements, suite dimensions, cuisine history — so AI engines can quote KTHP by name.
